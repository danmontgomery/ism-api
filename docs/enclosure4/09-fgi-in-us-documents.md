# DoDM 5200.01-V2 Enclosure 4: FGI Markings Used in U.S. Documents

## 9.1 General Rules

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-S9.b** | FGI requiring protection must be classified at least CONFIDENTIAL, unless noted in security agreements | S9.b |
| **E4-S9.d** | Use `FGI` with trigraphic country codes and international organization tetragraphs in the banner line | S9.d |
| **E4-S9.d.alpha** | Multiple country trigraphs listed alphabetically, followed by organization tetragraphs alphabetically, separated by single spaces | S9.d |
| **E4-S9.e** | If a specific government must be concealed, do not include country codes (use just `FGI` in banner/portion) | S9.e |
| **E4-S9.f** | FGI portions shall remain segregated from U.S. portions | S9.f |
| **E4-S9.g** | FGI from each individual country/organization shall remain segregated in separate portions | S9.g |
| **E4-S9.j** | Overall U.S. classification shall reflect the highest classification of any information, including FGI | S9.j |

## 9.2 FGI with NOFORN and REL TO

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-S9.k** | FGI and NOFORN in banner means document may not be disseminated to any foreign country without permission of U.S. originator and FGI source country | S9.k |
| **E4-S9.l** | REL TO cannot be used in overall classification of a document containing FGI portions UNLESS the entire document is releasable to all countries listed | S9.l |
| **E4-S9.l.precedence** | When both NOFORN and REL TO information are in the same document, NOFORN takes precedence over REL TO (in banner) | S9.l |
| **E4-S9.m** | Portion marks for FGI portions may NOT include NOFORN. NOFORN is a U.S. marking and not applicable to FGI-only portions. | S9.m |
