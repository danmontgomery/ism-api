# DoDM 5200.01-V2 Enclosure 4: Appendix 2 — DoS Dissemination Control Markings

>
> **Parent**: [dodm-5200.01-enclosure4-requirements.md](../dodm-5200.01-enclosure4-requirements.md)

---

## 13.1 EXDIS (Exclusive Distribution)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A2-S1.a** | EXDIS is a DoS marking used with classified information or CUI. Portion marking: `([classification]//XD)` | A2-S1.a |
| **E4-A2-S1.b** | EXDIS is used only for highly sensitive message traffic among the President, Secretary/Deputy Secretary/Under Secretaries of State, and DoS Chiefs of Mission | A2-S1.b |
| **E4-A2-S1.c** | Documents bearing EXDIS may NOT be released to foreign governments or international organizations | A2-S1.c |
| **E4-A2-S1.d** | EXDIS and NODIS may NOT be used together. Only one or the other on a document. | A2-S1.d |

## 13.2 NODIS (No Distribution)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A2-S2.a** | NODIS is a DoS marking for highest sensitivity messages. Portion marking: `([classification]//ND)` | A2-S2.a |
| **E4-A2-S2.d** | NODIS documents shall be administratively controlled as NOFORN; may not be released to foreign governments, foreign nationals, foreign organizations, or non-US citizens | A2-S2.d |
| **E4-A2-S2.e** | NODIS and EXDIS may NOT be used together. Only one or the other on a portion. | A2-S2.e |
| **E4-A2-S2.f** | NODIS has priority over EXDIS in the banner line | A2-S2.f |

## 13.3 SBU (Sensitive But Unclassified)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A2-S3.a** | SBU is a DoS marking for unclassified information. Banner: `UNCLASSIFIED//SBU`. Portion: `(U//SBU)` | A2-S3.a |
| **E4-A2-S3.b** | SBU shall be used only derivatively within DoD | A2-S3.b |

## 13.4 SBU-NF (Sensitive But Unclassified NOFORN)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A2-S4.a** | SBU-NF: unclassified DoS info protected + prohibited from dissemination to non-U.S. citizens. Banner: `UNCLASSIFIED//SBU NOFORN`. Portion: `(U//SBU-NF)` | A2-S4.a |
| **E4-A2-S4.b** | Used only with UNCLASSIFIED information. Derivatively only within DoD. | A2-S4.b |
