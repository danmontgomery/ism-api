## 4.1 General Format

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-S4.a.1** | All FGI classifications (banner and portion) shall begin with `//` | S4.a.(1) |
| **E4-S4.a.1a** | Required format: `//[country code] [equivalent classification]` | S4.a.(1) |
| **E4-S4.a.4** | FGI classifications shall NOT be annotated with U.S. classification markings or JOINT classification markings (mutually exclusive) | S4.a.(4) |

### NATO Constraints

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-S4.b.2.a** | COSMIC is the NATO designation for TOP SECRET; the word "NATO" is never used with TOP SECRET NATO info | S4.b.(2)(a) |
| **E4-S4.b.2.c** | BOHEMIA may be used ONLY with `//COSMIC TOP SECRET` | S4.b.(2)(c) |
| **E4-S4.b.3** | NATO banner markings may be used ONLY on NATO information | S4.b.(3) |
| **E4-S4.b.3a** | NOFORN cannot be used on NATO information | S4.b.(3) |
| **E4-S4.b.4** | When NATO info is incorporated into a U.S. document, the banner uses the highest U.S. or equivalent classification with `//FGI NATO` (e.g., `SECRET//FGI NATO`) | S4.b.(4) |

## 4.4 RESTRICTED and "In Confidence"

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-S4.c.1** | Foreign documents with RESTRICTED classification: mark as `//[country code] RESTRICTED` | S4.c.(1) |
| **E4-S4.c.2** | Additionally mark as `CONFIDENTIAL - Modified Handling` and protect per Volume 3, Enclosure 2, Section 17 | S4.c.(2) |
