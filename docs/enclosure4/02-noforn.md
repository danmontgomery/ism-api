# DoDM 5200.01-V2 Enclosure 4: NOFORN (Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals)

> **Source**: DoDM 5200.01-V2, February 24, 2012 (Change 4, 7/28/2020), Enclosure 4, Section 2
>
> **Parent**: [dodm-5200.01-enclosure4-requirements.md](../dodm-5200.01-enclosure4-requirements.md)

---

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-S2.a** | NOFORN identifies intelligence that may not be provided to foreign governments, international organizations, foreign nationals, or immigrant aliens without originator approval | S2.a |
| **E4-S2.b** | Within DoD, NOFORN is authorized ONLY on intelligence and intelligence-related information, with three exceptions: NNPI, NDP-1, and cover/cover support information per DoDI S-5105.63 | S2.b |
| **E4-S2.e** | NOFORN may be applied to unclassified intelligence information categorized as CUI | S2.e |
| **E4-S2.f** | NOFORN may not be applied to any other DoD information outside the identified exceptions | S2.f |
