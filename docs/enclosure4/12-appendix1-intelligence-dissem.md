# DoDM 5200.01-V2 Enclosure 4: Appendix 1 — Intelligence Dissemination Control Markings

>
> **Parent**: [dodm-5200.01-enclosure4-requirements.md](../dodm-5200.01-enclosure4-requirements.md)

---

## 12.1 IMCON (Controlled Imagery)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A1-S1.b** | IMCON may be applied ONLY to information classified at the SECRET level | A1-S1.b |
| **E4-A1-S1.e** | IMCON material must be classified SECRET | A1-S1.e |
| **E4-A1-S1.e.ts** | However, if IMCON information is in a paragraph containing TOP SECRET info, the appropriate classification is `TOP SECRET//IMCON` | A1-S1.e |
| **E4-A1-S1.f** | Banner of documents with both IMCON and NOFORN portions: `//IMCON/NOFORN`, classification no lower than SECRET | A1-S1.f |

## 12.2 NOFORN (Appendix 1 Detail)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A1-S2.b** | NOFORN shall NOT be applied to non-intelligence information (except NNPI, NDP-1, and cover/cover support) | A1-S2.b |
| **E4-A1-S2.c** | NOFORN may be used only with TOP SECRET, SECRET, CONFIDENTIAL, or CUI | A1-S2.c |
| **E4-A1-S2.d** | NOFORN cannot be used with REL TO or RELIDO in the banner line | A1-S2.d |
| **E4-A1-S2.d.precedence** | When a document contains NOFORN and REL TO or NOFORN and RELIDO portions, NOFORN takes precedence in banner | A1-S2.d |

## 12.3 PROPIN (Proprietary Information Involved)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A1-S3.b** | PROPIN may be used with TOP SECRET, SECRET, CONFIDENTIAL, or UNCLASSIFIED | A1-S3.b |
| **E4-A1-S3.c** | PROPIN appears in the banner if any portion contains PROPIN information | A1-S3.c |

## 12.4 RELIDO (Releasable by Information Disclosure Official)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A1-S4.b** | RELIDO may be used only with national intelligence information | A1-S4.b |
| **E4-A1-S4.c** | RELIDO may be used only with TOP SECRET, SECRET, or CONFIDENTIAL | A1-S4.c |
| **E4-A1-S4.c.rel** | RELIDO may be used independently or with REL TO | A1-S4.c |
| **E4-A1-S4.d** | RELIDO may NOT be used in the same portion or banner line with NOFORN | A1-S4.d |
| **E4-A1-S4.d.precedence** | When document contains both NOFORN and RELIDO portions, NOFORN takes precedence in banner | A1-S4.d |

## 12.5 FISA (Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act)

| ID | Requirement | Source |
|----|-------------|--------|
| **E4-A1-S5.a** | FISA denotes presence of FISA or FISA-derived information; informational marking | A1-S5.a |
| **E4-A1-S5.d** | Both banner and portion marking use abbreviation `FISA` | A1-S5.d |
